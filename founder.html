Our founder

Mary Morfin

For as long as I can remember, a red lip has been how I mark a special occasion: birthdays, weddings, a long-awaited night out. It's the finishing touch that makes the moment feel like an event.

What I couldn't find was a formula that did everything I wanted. Glosses were sticky, long-wear lipsticks were drying, and the most beautiful packaging rarely had the best color inside. So I started making my own.

Morfin Beauty begins with lips: a ruby gloss, a true red lipstick, a golden lip oil and a rose shimmer. Fragrance and face are next. Everything is made to feel as good as it looks, in packaging you'll want to leave out on your vanity.
