# Glossary

## Morfin Beauty
The brand and its product line of luxury beauty products. The website exists to present Morfin Beauty.

## Founder
Mary Morfin, who founded Morfin Beauty. Always "the Founder" in copy about the brand's origin.

## Collection
A grouping of Morfin Beauty products by type. There are three: the **Lip Collection** (lipstick, lip gloss, lip oil), the **Fragrance Collection** (perfume), and the **Face Collection** (foundation, blush, highlighter). The Lip Collection is the brand's flagship. Avoid "category" in customer-facing language.

## Tagline
"Luxury Lips. Lasting Impression." The brand's single primary tagline, used site-wide regardless of Collection.

## Inner Circle
The Morfin Beauty mailing list. Joining it ("Join the Inner Circle") is how visitors hear about launches. A **Notify Me** request is an Inner Circle sign-up tied to one specific product.

## Monogram
The interlocked gold "MB" mark. The **sparkle monogram** (MB with a four-point star) is the primary logo; the **crown monogram** (MB topped by a crown) is an accent mark only.
