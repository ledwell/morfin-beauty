Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days. Delivery times are estimates from the day your order ships. We currently ship within the United States.

Returns

We want you to love every piece. If something isn't right, unopened and unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method.

For hygiene reasons, opened cosmetics can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or faulty.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number, and we'll make it right.

How to start a return

  1. Email hello@morfinbeauty.net with your order number.
  2. We'll send a return label and instructions.
  3. Your refund is issued within 5–7 business days of the return arriving.
